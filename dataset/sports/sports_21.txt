Virat Kohli should be given a three-match ban and he should not be allowed to play in the Ahmedabad Tests against England, said former English cricketer, coach and commentator David Lloyd. Writing in his column for Daily Mail, Lloyd said: “(How can) the captain of a national team be allowed to criticise, berate, intimidate and ridicule an official on the pitch? Kohli certainly shouldn’t be playing in Ahmedabad next week. (He should miss) the next three Tests.”
