If the pitch for the Day-Night Test at Motera behaves on the same lines as the one for the second Chennai Test, it would neither surprise nor disappoint Rohit Sharma. The Indian opener, who hit a match-setting 161 in the first innings on a rank-turner to help the team get back on terms in the series, sees nothing wrong in teams exploiting home advantage in terms of pitch and conditions. He argued that both teams have the opportunity to utilise the surface on offer. The pitch for the second Test aided spinners from the start, with the Indian trio of Ravichandran Ashwin, Axar Patel and Kuldeep Yadav much more adept at using it than their England counterparts, Jack Leach and Moeen Ali. It’s a formula that is likely to be carried forward in the pink-ball Test, which usually is dominated by seam and swing bowlers. Rohit doesn’t see anything wrong in it.
