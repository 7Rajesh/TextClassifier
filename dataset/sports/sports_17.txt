IPL franchise Rajasthan Royals on Sunday parted ways with head coach Andrew McDonald after they mutually agreed “to move in different directions with regards to the leadership structure”.
The inaugural champions also announced that they have drafted in former Zimbabwe cricketer Trevor Penney as the lead assistant coach for the upcoming season.
“Everyone at Rajasthan Royals would like to thank Andrew for his efforts towards the IPL season 13,” Jake Lush McCrum, COO, Rajasthan Royals was quoted as saying in a press release by the franchise.
